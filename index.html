OUR PARTNER ECOSYSTEM

One accountable lead.
The right partners to deliver.

OP2MUM brings together investors, experienced executives, industry experts and reliable delivery partners. We assemble the right team for each project, combining commercial, financial, engineering and digital capabilities to move from development to execution.

  • Investors & Finance

    Capital strategy, financial modelling and investor engagement. Our network helps shape the funding approach, strengthen the investment case and prepare projects for due diligence and investment decisions.

  • Executives & Experts

    Experienced executives and sector specialists bring leadership, commercial judgement and market knowledge. They support partnership building, negotiations and the decisions needed to move complex projects forward.

  • Engineering Partners

    Specialist engineering partners support feasibility, technical design, technology selection and systems integration. OP2MUM coordinates their work to define scope, manage interfaces and prepare projects for delivery.

  • Digital & AI Partners

    Partners combine AI, data and digital engineering to improve decisions, automate workflows and connect systems. They support solution design, integration and deployment, helping teams put technology into everyday business operations.

EXPERIENCE AT THE HELM

International perspective. Hands-on leadership.

Chief Executive Officer

Langtry Meyer

Founder and developer of energy projects. International business builder.

Langtry founded Texas LNG and Texas Green Fuels, leading complex energy infrastructure projects from initial concept through engineering, permitting, commercial development, and strategic investment. His experience includes securing sites, assembling international development teams, raising development capital, and bringing projects into institutional ownership.

His broader career includes senior roles with Samsung Group and Majid Al Futtaim, building businesses and partnerships across international markets. He combines hands-on project development experience with commercial judgment and a practical understanding of how sponsors, technology providers, contractors, and investors work together.

At OP2MUM, Langtry leads client relationships and project development, bringing the partners, agreements, and capital strategy together to advance projects toward execution.
